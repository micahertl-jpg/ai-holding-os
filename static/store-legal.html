What you're buying

Each report sold here is produced by an automated AI research process, not by a human analyst, and is delivered to the email address you provide, usually within a few minutes of payment. Every field in a report is an estimate with an explicit confidence level (low, medium, or high) — never a guarantee. Reports are a starting point for your own judgment and research, not financial, legal, investment, or other professional advice, and should not be relied on as the sole basis for a business, financial, or legal decision.

Refund policy

If your order has not yet been delivered — for example, if the research or email step fails or is taking unreasonably long — contact us and we will refund you in full, no questions asked.

Once a report has been delivered to your email, the sale is final. Because this is digital content generated specifically for your requested topic, we're not able to offer refunds after delivery except where required by applicable law, or at our discretion in individual cases (for example, if the report is genuinely unusable due to an error on our end).

To request a refund or report a problem with an order, email Agency33ai@outlook.com with your order confirmation and a description of the issue. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe handles all payment data directly, under its own security and compliance standards.

What information we collect and why

  • Your email address — used solely to deliver your report and to respond if you contact us about an order. We do not sell or share it with third parties for marketing.
  • The topic you submit — used to generate your report and, in aggregate/anonymized form, may help us understand what kinds of reports are useful. We do not publish your specific submitted topic.
  • Payment details — collected and processed entirely by Stripe; we only receive confirmation that payment succeeded, plus the amount and currency.

We use a small number of third-party services to operate this store: Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery), and Anthropic's Claude models (report generation). Each processes the minimum data needed to perform its function on our behalf.
